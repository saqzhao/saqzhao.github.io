Hello! I am currently a Fulbright Fellow at EMBL Heidelberg (2025-2026) studying computional prediction of intraproteome interactions and high-throughput genetics of bacterial species in the gut microbiome in an effort to characterise them as model species. I graduated from MIT (2024 BS, 2025 MEng), where I studied computational biology and evolutionary genomics under Prof. Manolis Kellis, Ph.D. In August 2026, I will matriculate as a PhD student and Skaggs-Oxford Scholar at Scripps Research Institute and Oxford University with an interest in virology, particularly host-pathogen interactions and viral evolution.
